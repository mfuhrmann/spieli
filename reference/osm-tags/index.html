    OSM Tags spieli Reads¶

    Which OpenStreetMap tags change what spieli shows, and how. This is the contract between mappers and the map: if a playground looks wrong in spieli, the tags below are what to check. All rules are implemented in importer/api.sql (the playground_stats view), some mirrored in the frontend; a change to a rule changes this page.

    Why the rules are what they are is explained elsewhere: Mapping detail, User Guide → Wheelchair accessibility.

    Playgrounds and what belongs to them¶

    What Tag Notes
    A playground leisure=playground, as an area or a node A node is treated as a circle of 5 m radius, so only equipment within 5 m of it counts — map the area where you can
    Its equipment any object whose geometry intersects the playground area Points inside, or ways and areas overlapping it
    Trees natural=tree nodes and natural=tree_row ways around the playground Drawn when the playground is open; its tree count uses natural=tree within 15 m of the area
    A fence around it see Fenced below
    A pitch outside any playground leisure=pitch not intersecting a playground area Shown on the Sports pitches (outside playgrounds) layer

    What each device, fitness station and pitch is called and how it is grouped: Equipment Types.

    Mapping detail¶

    Two criteria, each met by any one qualifying tag:

    Criterion Met when
    Play equipment Inside the area there is an object tagged playground=*, or a leisure=pitch with sport=soccer, basketball/streetball or table_tennis
    Info The playground area carries opening_hours, surface, or access with a value other than yes

    Both → detailed, one → basic, neither → no details yet. Not counted: benches, shelters and picnic tables (amenity=bench, amenity=shelter, leisure=picnic_table), and photos.

    Who may use it¶

    From access on the playground area:

    access spieli shows Hidden by Public only Hidden by No private ones
    unset, yes, anything else public no no
    customers for customers & guests only (cup badge) yes no
    private, no private (grey pin, lock) yes yes

    Filters¶

    Filter Matches when
    Water playground A device inside has a playground value containing water, or playground=splash_pad / pump — (or, when nothing is mapped separately, playground:<device>=yes / a count on the area itself)
    Baby-friendly A device inside has baby=yes, or is playground=baby_swing, basketswing or sandpit, or carries capacity:baby — (or, when nothing is mapped separately, playground:<device>=yes / a count on the area itself). A spring rider counts for toddlers, not babies (#1075)
    Toddler-friendly A device inside — any device, e.g. a small structure or playhouse — has provided_for:toddler=yes, or is playground=springy or basketswing — (or, when nothing is mapped separately, playground:<device>=yes / a count on the area itself)
    Wheelchair-friendly A play device (playground=*) inside carries wheelchair=yes, limited or designated; a playground=sandpit counts only with wheelchair=yes. The area's own wheelchair tag, benches and pitches do not count. If devices carry wheelchair tags but none qualifies, the panel says No wheelchair equipment; if none carries one, it says nothing. A sandpit tagged anything other than wheelchair=yes does not count as surveyed, so a playground whose only tagged device is such a sandpit shows nothing either
    With bench amenity=bench inside
    With picnic table leisure=picnic_table inside
    With shelter amenity=shelter inside
    Table tennis table leisure=pitch + sport=table_tennis inside
    With football pitch leisure=pitch + sport=soccer inside
    Basketball court leisure=pitch + sport=basketball or streetball inside
    Fenced The area has enclosed=yes or barrier=fence, or separately mapped barrier=fence ways run within 4 m of at least 75 % of its outline
    Dogs allowed dog=yes on the area
    Themed An allowlisted playground:theme on the area or on a device inside (see below)
    With shade shade=yes or shade=partial on the area (#1072). The panel reads the tag itself: yes Shaded, partial Partly shaded, no No shade, untagged nothing
    Lit at night lit on the area reads as lit (see below)

    Values separated by ; are split for sport and playground:theme.

    Area-level equipment¶

    Equipment can also be recorded on the playground area itself: playground:<device>=yes, or a count (playground:swing=2). spieli reads it for the devices in its catalogue only — playground:theme, playground:type, playground:surface and other sub-keys are not equipment — and only as a fallback: when at least one device is mapped as its own object, the area tags are ignored, so a playground mapped both ways is not counted twice. It then counts for mapping detail ("has equipment"), the water, baby and toddler filters, and the panel's activities and highlights (#1074).

    Activities and highlights (detail panel)¶

    The panel's What kids can do row and its highlights line are derived from the play devices inside the playground — separately mapped playground=* objects and playground:<device>=yes|<n> on the area itself. Each device type in app/src/lib/objPlaygroundEquipment.js declares its activity and whether it is a highlight:

    Activity Devices (playground=*)
    Swing swing, baby_swing, basketswing, tire_swing, rope_swing
    Slide slide
    Rock seesaw, springy
    Climb climbingframe, climbingwall, climbing_slope, climbing_pole, monkey_bars, climbing, mound, dome
    Balance balancebeam, rope_traverse, stepping_stone, stepping_post, balance, agility_trail, wobble_bridge, belt_bridge
    Spin roundabout, basketrotator, aerialrotator, spinner, spinning_disc, spinning_circle, spinner_bowl, rotator
    Bounce trampoline, cushion, spring_board, hopscotch
    Exercise horizontal_bar, parallel_bars, bannister_bars, hamster_wheel, exercise
    Dig sandpit, chute, sieve, sand_wheel, sand_seesaw, sand_pulley, excavator, table, sand
    Explore playhouse, tunnel_tube

    Structure parts (structure, platform, ladder, steps, bridge) and water devices are no activity.

    Highlights, in this order: the playground's theme; Water play once for any water device (category water, or the server's water flag); then, in catalogue order, aerialrotator, hamster_wheel, zipwire, trampoline, speaking_tube, funnel_ball, ball_pool, marble_run, musical_instrument, sledding.

    Lighting (lit)¶

    Read case-insensitively, on playgrounds and on standalone pitches alike:

    Value Read as
    yes, 24/7, automatic, limited, interval lit
    any value containing a digit, sunset or sunrise (e.g. sunset-22:00) lit
    no, disused not lit
    unset, or anything else unknown — shown as nothing

    Themes (playground:theme)¶

    Only these values count: ship, castle, spiderweb, water, adventure, rocket, dragon, octopus, circus. Others — including the shape of a single spring rider (horse, duck, …) — are ignored. On the area they produce a banner in the panel; on a device, a symbol next to it.

    Photos¶

    The camera marker on the map, and the panel's photo section, come from tags on the playground:

    Tag Shown as
    panoramax, panoramax:* street-level photos (Panoramax)
    wikimedia_commons Commons gallery (a file or a category)
    image pointing at a Wikimedia/Wikipedia file (an upload. URL or an image file extension) Commons gallery

    An image link to anywhere else, or to a wiki page, is ignored. Photos are not part of mapping detail.

    Region boundary¶

    The region of a data-node is the OSM relation in OSM_RELATION_ID (usually a boundary=administrative relation). It is used to cut the region out of the extract and for the region's name and outline in get_meta; playgrounds are not filtered against the boundary afterwards.
